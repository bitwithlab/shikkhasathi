# Roles and Panels

## Student
Discover courses, enroll, watch lessons, track progress, take exams, submit
assignments and manage profile/notifications.

## Seller / Instructor
Create and manage courses, lessons, pricing, resources, student engagement and
sales/earning reports, subject to platform review and permissions.

## Administrator
Manage users, universities, departments, courses, sellers, orders, payments,
refunds, moderation, platform settings, reports and audit logs.

All role permissions must be checked by the backend API.
